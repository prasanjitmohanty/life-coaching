What is life coaching?

Life coaching is a collaborative conversation focused on your goals, choices, and actions. It offers space to reflect, explore possibilities, and decide on practical steps. You remain the person making decisions about your life.

Do I need a clear goal before I start?

No. An unanswered question, a period of change, or a sense that you want something to be different can be a starting point. Clarifying what you want to focus on can be part of the conversation.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is not mental health treatment and does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions. If you need support for psychological distress or a mental health concern, a qualified mental health professional is the appropriate person to contact.

Will someone tell me what to do?

The intention is not to prescribe a life plan or give you a set of "right" answers. Coaching uses questions and reflection to help you consider your options and choose actions that fit your circumstances and values.
